How it fits together

1 · The data is all free and public

Prices (Finnhub/yfinance), filings (SEC EDGAR), and fund holdings (13F) are free, machine-readable feeds. Bloomberg's moat isn't the data — it's packaging it in one place. You can do the packaging yourself.

2 · A database is your terminal's memory

Pull the feeds into your own SQLite database and you can ask questions across everything at once — instead of clicking one ticker at a time on a free website.

3 · Claude is the interface

You don't write the plumbing — you describe what you want ("download these filings, store them, chart this, flag that") and Claude Code writes the scripts. That's genuinely how ours is built. It also drives TradingView directly for the charting layer.
