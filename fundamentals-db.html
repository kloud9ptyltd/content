Compounder Screen

A stock screener built from free SEC filings.

Every US public company is required by law to report its numbers to the SEC every quarter — and the SEC publishes all of it, free, no signup. This screener pulls those raw filings for 3,000+ companies, scores each one 0–100 on durable revenue growth, and ranks the whole universe so the strongest businesses float to the top. Data bill: $0.

The build — 4 stages

1 PULL — straight from the source

The SEC's own servers hand out every company's full filing history as clean JSON. No vendor, no subscription, no scraping:

https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK##########.json
This is the same underlying data Bloomberg terminals charge $25k/yr to show you. (The SEC's filing system is called EDGAR — that's the only jargon you need.)

2 PARSE — make every company comparable

Filings are messy: fiscal years differ, Q4 is buried inside the annual report (so it's derived as full year minus Q1+Q2+Q3), and one-off spikes get cleaned out. The result: an apples-to-apples quarterly revenue history for every name.

3 SCORE — the exact recipe (0–100)

The score rewards companies that grow revenue relentlessly, for years — not one hot quarter:

25Durable growth rate — multi-year revenue compounding, not a one-year pop
22Consistency — does trailing-12-month revenue rise almost every quarter?
20Recent growth — still growing strongly year-over-year right now
15Track record — how many years it's been proving it
8Acceleration — growth speeding up beats slowing down
10Margins & profit — quality check, kept minor on purpose
Stock price is deliberately not chased — it only nudges the final score ±5. The point is finding proven revenue machines, whether or not the stock already popped.

4 RANK — junk is kicked out first

Before ranking, the screen removes what fakes growth: crypto miners, commodity cyclicals (their revenue is just the commodity price), companies growing off a tiny base, sub-scale names under $50M revenue, and $200B+ mega-caps (too big to 5-10x).

Build your own — one prompt

💬 Paste this into Claude

The whole thing is buildable in an afternoon with an AI coding assistant:

Build me a Python stock screener that uses the SEC's free companyfacts API (data.sec.gov, no key needed — just set a User-Agent header). Pull quarterly revenue for all US companies over $2B market cap, derive Q4 as full-year minus Q1-Q3, store in SQLite, then score each company 0-100: multi-year revenue CAGR (25 pts), consistency of TTM revenue rising each quarter (22), recent YoY growth (20), years of track record (15), acceleration (8), margins (10). Exclude crypto miners, commodity cyclicals, and sub-$50M-revenue names. Output the top 20 as a table.
Respect the SEC's fair-use rules: identify yourself in the User-Agent and keep requests under ~10/sec.

! Read this before acting on any screen

A screen is a starting point, not a buy list. High scores mean the past revenue was durable — they say nothing about valuation, competition, or what happens next. Research every name yourself. Educational only, not financial advice.
