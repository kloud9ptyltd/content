07 · Good to know

Honest notes.

The Bloomberg Terminal is widely reported at roughly $30K a year per seat. Free data is often delayed and has thinner coverage, which is fine for learning and research but not for live trading.
A Columbia or Princeton master's buys you recruiting access and a network. The free courses teach the same math, but they don't give you the on-campus recruiting pipeline.
The CFA is still a credential employers recognise. Damodaran teaches the skills, not the letters after your name.
MATLAB is still used at some banks and in some research groups. Python is the default at most quant shops today.
NeetCode, QuantGuide and Hugging Face are freemium. Everything linked here is in the free tier.

The expensive version is optional. The work isn't.
