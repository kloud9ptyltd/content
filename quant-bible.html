Try 3 of the real questions
Jane Street · Round 1

Roll a die, you get paid the number. You may re-roll once. What's the fair price of this game?

Reveal answer
One roll alone is worth 3.5. With a free re-roll you only re-roll when the first roll is below 3.5 — so keep 4, 5, 6, re-roll 1, 2, 3. That's ½ × 3.5 + ⅙ × (4+5+6) = $4.25.
Asked constantly in trading phone screens

1000 coins: 999 fair, one has heads on both sides. You pick one at random and flip 10 heads in a row. Odds you're holding the rigged coin?

Reveal answer
Feels like a near-certainty — it's a coin flip. Bayes' theorem: 1 / (1 + 999/1024) ≈ 50%. The rigged coin is rare enough that 999 fair coins produce a 10-heads streak about as often as the one rigged coin does.
Citadel

100 passengers board a plane in order; passenger 1 is drunk and sits randomly. Everyone else takes their own seat if free, random if taken. Odds passenger 100 gets their own seat?

Reveal answer
Every random choice either ends the chaos (seat 1) or passes it on — and seats 1 and 100 are always equally likely to be the one that gets taken. Exactly 1/2.
