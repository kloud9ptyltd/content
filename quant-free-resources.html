3 free things quant firms hand you

Trading firms publish a surprising amount of their own prep material. These are the three from the reel, with a link and what each one is good for. All links checked 17 November 2026.

01 · From the reel

From the reel

01 · Jane Street on YouTube

Jane Street mock interviews

What it is: Two full mock interviews run by Jane Street staff, “A Jane Street Trading Mock Interview with Graham and Andrea” and “A Jane Street Software Engineering Mock Interview with Grace and Nolen”.

Use it for: hearing how a strong candidate talks through a question before your own interview.

Trading, 25 min ↗Software engineering, 37 min ↗
02 · Optiver

Optiver on Kaggle

What it is: Two competitions built on real market data: predict closing price moves for US stocks, and predict short-term volatility. Both competitions have ended; the data, notebooks and discussion are still on the page.

Use it for: a market-data modelling project you can put on a resume.

Trading at the Close ↗Realized Volatility ↗
03 · Akuna Capital

Akuna Options 101

What it is: Akuna's free course covering industry fundamentals such as open outcry, electronic trading and option greeks.

Use it for: learning options from an options market maker.
