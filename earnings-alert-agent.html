Earnings alert agentStarter brief
Build an always-on earnings alert job for my NAS. It should: • scan a reputable finance API for today’s pre-market and after-hours earnings • rank the most market-relevant companies and create a compact daily preview • monitor releases, compare actual EPS/revenue with forecasts, and report beat/miss/in-line • include an indicative stock reaction and refresh it shortly afterward • support my choice of Discord, Telegram, or WhatsApp • archive results, prevent duplicate alerts, retry safely, and keep secrets in environment variables Before implementation, inspect what can be reused on the NAS and show me the proposed architecture, schedule, data sources, and test plan. Do not expose API keys or webhook secrets.
Keep it honest: free market-data feeds can be delayed, incomplete or revised. Treat alerts as a fast research aid, verify important releases at the source, and never commit credentials to code.
