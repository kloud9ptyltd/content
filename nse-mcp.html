Free setup guide · Updated 18 Nov 2026

Ask the stock exchange anything

On 8 November 2026 the National Stock Exchange of India opened two free MCP servers. Connect them to Claude or ChatGPT once and you can ask about any NSE stock in plain English: prices, 52-week ranges, dividends, sector moves, index valuations. The AI pulls the numbers from NSE's own data. Below: the two-minute setup, 20 prompts to copy, real answers we got, and what it can't do.

01 · What it is

A plug between NSE and your AI

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is a standard way for AI apps to reach outside data. NSE built the socket; Claude or ChatGPT plugs in and looks up NSE data whenever your question needs it. No account, no API key, no code.

History · 21 tools

nse-bhavcopy

End-of-day prices for 3,500+ stocks over 5 years, plus indices, valuations, breadth, India VIX and corporate actions. Updated after each close.

02 · Connect it

Setup in two minutes

You need an AI app that supports custom MCP connectors: Claude on a Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise plan, or a paid ChatGPT plan. The NSE side is free.

  1. 1

    Claude: open Connectors

    In Claude (web or desktop), go to Settings → Connectors.

  2. 2

    Add the history server

    Click Add custom connector. Name it nse-bhavcopy, paste the URL below, then click Add.

    https://mcp.nseindia.in/bhavcopy/cm/mcp
  3. 3

    Add the live server

    Repeat with the name cm-market. Leave Advanced settings blank.

    https://mcp.nseindia.in/cmmkt/mcp
  4. 4

    Switch them on

    Start a new chat, click + → Connectors and turn on both NSE connectors. On Team and Enterprise plans an organisation owner adds them in Admin settings first.

  5. 5

    ChatGPT instead?

    On a paid plan, go to Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced settings and turn on Developer mode. Then Create a connector for each URL above, with no authentication. Menu names change between app versions, so follow the closest match.

  6. 6

    Test it

    NSE's suggested first question:

    How did the market close today? Show the top 5 stocks by turnover.

05 · Honest limits

What it can't do (yet)

No intraday candles, ticks or order book. History is end of day; live data is a snapshot 1–3 minutes behind.
No F&O. We found no option-chain, futures or FII/DII flow tools on either server.
Old prices are not adjusted for splits or bonuses. For long periods add "check for splits or bonuses" to your question.
3 months per history call. The AI makes more calls on its own for longer periods, so a 1-year question takes a little longer.
Holidays are handled. "Last trading session" works better than "yesterday".
Informational use only. NSE's terms bar commercial use and training AI models on the data.
THE HONEST LINE

It makes looking things up effortless. It doesn't make the AI right about what happens next. Use it to check facts fast, then do your own thinking.
