01 · Jane Street

Jane Street: first year of university.

Both programs are paid for by Jane Street: travel, meals, housing and social events. Neither needs a finance background. Dates aren't posted yet, so use "Sign up to be notified" on each page.

01
Jane Street · First year

FOCUS

ALL EXPENSES PAIDMULTI-DAY1ST YEAR

A multi-day program to meet Jane Street's trading, software and strategy teams, with travel, meals, housing and a daily allowance covered. For first-year students who've faced barriers to advanced STEM opportunities; their definition is broad (being first in your family at university counts).

02
Jane Street · First year · STEM

FTTP

ALL EXPENSES PAIDA FEW DAYSNO FINANCE NEEDED

A few days in the office: classes on how Jane Street trades, then a team mock-trading game. For first-year students planning to study maths, physics, computer science or another STEM subject.

Discover Citadel

FLIGHTS + HOTEL PAIDNEW YORK · EARLY APR 2027DEADLINE 5 MAR 2027

A short in-person event inside Citadel in New York for first- and second-year undergraduates (graduating December 2028 to June 2030). Citadel covers a round-trip economy flight, ground transport, two hotel nights and meals on the competition day, worth about $750. You'll hear by 12 March 2027.

05 · Good to know

Honest notes.

None of these guarantee an internship. They get you inside the firm and in front of the people who hire. Optiver's is the only one that says it can fast-track an offer.
Check you're eligible. Jane Street FOCUS is for students who've faced barriers to STEM; Citadel wants first- or second-years; Optiver's open edition is for students in the UK, Ireland or EU graduating in 2029.
Citadel's benefits are taxable to you, per its program terms. The flight and hotel are booked for you.
