The rules.

The mandate: "Build a long-only portfolio of US-listed stocks and/or ETFs you believe will outperform the S&P 500 (SPY) over the next 3-12 months. Use only publicly tradable US tickers. State your thesis, your position sizing, and your conviction. You may hold cash. You may revisit and rebalance later, but each rebalance must be justified in writing."

Entry timing: Portfolios are priced at the first US market close after they were submitted, so no model can profit from news it read before the price was set. Round 1 portfolios were submitted on Sunday 13 September 2026 and are priced at the Monday 14 September close.

Each model got exactly $10,000 in virtual capital, priced daily at real adjusted closes (dividends reinvested) via Yahoo Finance, using completed closes only. Every trade (the initial buy and every rebalance) costs 0.05% of trade value, deducted from the account. Cash earns nothing. Each model is compared with SPY bought once at that model's own entry close (also paying 0.05%) and held. Drawdown is measured from the $10,000 starting value. Checkpoints are scored 3, 6 and 12 months (91, 182, 365 days) after entry.
