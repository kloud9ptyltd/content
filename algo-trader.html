How to use this page

  1. Watch the Learn video for a skill. Just watch, no notes needed.
  2. Do the practice project under it. Copy the prompt into ChatGPT or Claude and let the AI walk you through it step by step.

Never coded before? Do the skills in this order: 04 Python → 03 Charting → 02 Backtesting → 05 Risk → 01 Trading bots. You can use Google Colab (free, runs in your browser) for every coding project, so there's nothing to install.

Your project

Build a simple bot that trades SPY on a free paper account (fake money): buy when the price is above its 50-day average, sell when it drops below.

  1. Open a free Alpaca paper trading account and copy your paper API keys.
  2. Open Google Colab or install Python, then paste the prompt below into ChatGPT or Claude.
  3. Run the bot once a day and check the paper orders in your Alpaca dashboard.
  4. Optional: run TradingAgents on one ticker to watch a team of AI agents argue about a trade. It needs an API key from an AI provider, and each run costs a little.
Copy into ChatGPT or Claude
You are my patient coding tutor and I'm a beginner. Help me build a simple trading bot in Python that ONLY uses Alpaca's paper trading account (fake money).

Rules: once a day, check SPY. If the latest close is above its 50-day moving average and I have no position, buy 1 share. If it's below and I hold SPY, sell it.

Use the alpaca-py library, read my API keys from environment variables (never hard-code them), print what the bot decided and why, and log every order to a CSV file.

Explain each block of code in plain English, then tell me exactly how to run it step by step.

You're done when: the bot prints its decision, and the paper order shows up in your Alpaca dashboard.

Act as my quant tutor. Using Python with yfinance and backtesting.py, write a beginner-friendly script that:

1. Downloads daily SPY prices from 2010 to today.
2. Tests a strategy that buys when the 50-day moving average crosses above the 200-day, and sells when it crosses below.

Copy into ChatGPT or Claude
I'm learning to read stock charts. I've attached a screenshot of a daily candlestick chart I marked up.

Act as a strict but kind trading coach:
1. Describe the trend in one sentence.
2. Check whether my support and resistance lines are in the right places and suggest better levels.
3. Point out any candlestick patterns and what the volume is telling us.
4. Tell me what beginners usually get wrong on a chart like this.

You're done when: you've marked up 5 charts and your levels mostly match the coach's.

Copy into ChatGPT or Claude
Help me build a position-size calculator in Python for paper trading.

Inputs: account size, the % of my account I'm willing to lose on one trade (default 1%), entry price and stop-loss price.
Outputs: how many shares to buy, the dollar amount at risk, and the position as a % of my account. Warn me if the position would be more than 20% of the account.

Then simulate 100 trades with a 45% win rate, where winners make 2x the risk and losers lose 1x. Show how the account changes when I risk 1% vs 5% vs 10% per trade, on a chart.

Explain in plain English what this teaches about the risk of ruin.

You're done when: you can explain why the 10% version blows up even with the same win rate.
