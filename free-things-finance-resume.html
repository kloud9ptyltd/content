02 · Good to know

Honest notes.

These are completion certificates and badges, not professional qualifications. They show initiative and give you something specific to talk about; they will not get you hired on their own.
List Forage items as "job simulation" certificates under courses or extracurriculars. Forage asks that you do not list them as work experience or an internship.
Akuna Options 101 is a free course with no certificate. List it as a completed course.
Google does not show a price before you sign in. Google Skills offers some courses free and puts others behind credits or a subscription, so check after signing in.
WorldQuant's Data Science Lab is the biggest commitment here: about 16 weeks, with an entry quiz.
Forage simulations get retired without notice. The J.P. Morgan and Citi ones were taken down. If a link says "no longer available", pick another from the list.
