The framework · how FRIDAY grades

Timing beats opinions.

1
The tide

Is the primary trend with you or against you? Being right against the tide is how famous investors lose billions — the market can stay irrational longer than you can stay solvent.

2
The crowd

Who still has to buy? When a name is the loudest thing on your feed and every fund already owns it, the future buyers are gone — you're the exit liquidity.

3
The clock

Story stocks run on catalysts with real dates — permits, launches, capital raises, earnings. "Too Early" isn't "wrong"; it means the clock says wait.

Straight talk

These are timing grades, not price targets — the desk's editorial read on where each name sits in its cycle, graded from public information. A "Too Late" can grade "Right Now" after a real reset (that's why the board re-grades every week), and a "Right Now" can be vetoed the moment the story changes.
