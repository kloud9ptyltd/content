The AI Trading Floor
Free · open source

A pixel office where nine AI agents run your trading floor.

Not a dashboard — an actual living office. A team of AI agents sit at their desks, talk to each other, hand off work, and run the desk while you watch. We reskinned it into the Cloud9 Trading Desk — same open-source engine, our cast of agents.

The desk — nine specialists

A coordinator runs the floor and delegates to nine agents across three wings:

  • Athena · fundamentals
  • Chartist · charts & levels
  • Oracle · quant & models
  • Atlas · macro
  • Capitol · Congress & 13F
  • Scout · movers & catalysts
  • Sentinel · risk (the gate)
  • Pilot · execution (paper)
  • Ledger · the book & P&L

Each agent is a real CLI coding agent (Claude, Codex or Gemini) — they read files, run commands, and work autonomously toward a goal. Sentinel gates every trade; execution is paper-only.

It's open source — make it yours

🛠️ Built on an open engine, tuned for Cloud9

The floor runs on the open-source Munder Difflin multi-agent harness (MIT, by Chaitanya Giri). We fine-tuned it into the Cloud9 Trading Desk — renamed the cast, added a live ticker and market signage, wired the agents to finance data. You can do the same: fork it and shape the agents, roles and goals around your workflow — coding team, research desk, content studio, whatever you run.
