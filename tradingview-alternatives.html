3 free TradingView alternatives
(no broker, no KYC).

TradingView's free plan now caps you at 30 drawings per chart and 2 indicators, and some desktop users are getting an upgrade ad with no close button. These three are free, need no broker account, and you can open them in a browser today.

How to use this page

  1. Open each link in a browser tab.
  2. Try your usual setup on one chart for 10 minutes.
  3. Keep the one that fits; you don't have to pick just one.
01 · Tool

#1 OpenMarket: replay and pro options data

What's freeCharts, indicators and drawings ("free to use" per the site), tick-by-tick market replay, and a BTC options panel (data from Deribit): GEX profile, max pain, call/put walls, expected move, put/call ratio.
Try this firstOpen BTCUSDT, start Replay on yesterday's session at 10x, and mark where price reacted to the max-pain and call-wall levels.

AI prompt · about 20 minutes

Copy-paste prompt

Copy into ChatGPT or Claude
Explain gamma exposure (GEX) and max pain like I'm a beginner. Then give me a 5-step checklist to read a GEX profile on BTC before I take a trade, and 3 mistakes beginners make with it.

Done when: You can say, for today, where the biggest call wall and put wall are and whether dealers are long or short gamma.

02 · Tool

#2 DM Charts: order flow with zero sign-up

What's freeNo sign-up or login. "No limit on indicators or panes" (per the site). Pine Editor that runs TradingView Pine Scripts, footprint charts, heatmap and a DOM ladder. Crypto, stocks, FX, commodities.
Try this firstOpen BTCUSDT, add the DOM ladder and footprint, and watch where big bids or asks sit before price moves.
Open DM Charts ↗
AI prompt · about 20 minutes

Copy-paste prompt

Copy into ChatGPT or Claude
I'm new to order flow. Explain a DOM ladder, footprint chart and delta in plain English, then give me one simple setup to practice spotting absorption on a 1-minute chart.

Done when: You've pasted one of your TradingView Pine Scripts into the Pine Editor and it plots on the chart.

03 · Tool

#3 Vela by LuxAlgo: unlimited drawings + a built-in quant AI

What's freeFree account, no card. Unlimited drawings, up to 16 charts per tab, every LuxAlgo tool (incl. Smart Money Concepts) built in, Pine editor, paper trading, and a one-click "Bring my TradingView setup" import. iPhone and Android apps.
The AIQuant AI can write indicators, build strategies and run a backtest agent loop ("Strategy", "Backtest agent loop", "Fix my code" modes).
Open sourceThe chart engine is on GitHub (github.com/LuxAlgo/Vela, Apache-2.0).
Try this firstImport your TradingView setup, then ask Quant AI: "Build a 50/200 moving-average crossover strategy on SPY daily and backtest it."

Good to know

Honest notes.

Free tiers change. Everything here was checked on Nov 26, 2026.
Vela's Quant AI has limited free usage; heavier use needs a paid plan.
Imports can break. Vela's TradingView import and PineTS may fail on some complex scripts. Simple indicators usually come over fine.
OpenMarket's options panel covers crypto options (Deribit), not stock options. It also has its own scripting language.
DM Charts' footer says "Charts by Vela", so it appears to use Vela's open-source chart engine; it's a separate app.
TradingView's free Basic plan still includes the Strategy Tester; the limits are 30 drawings per chart, 2 indicators, 1 chart per tab and 3 alerts (per TradingView's pricing page).
The no-close-button ad is from user reports on the Windows/Mac desktop app, not every account.
