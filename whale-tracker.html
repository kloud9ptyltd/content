The idea in 60 seconds

1 · The data is sitting there

The SEC publishes 13F filings for free at sec.gov (EDGAR) — including bulk machine-readable datasets. Anyone can download them.

2 · A database beats a website

Free 13F websites show you one fund at a time. Load the filings into your own database and you can ask questions across all funds at once — which stocks are being added, quarter over quarter, by how many funds. That's the whole trick.

3 · Claude writes the code

You don't need to be a programmer. Describe what you want to Claude Code — "download these filings, store them, show me quarter-over-quarter changes" — and it builds the scripts. That's genuinely how we built ours.

⚠ Honest limitations

13Fs arrive 45 days after quarter-end and show only long US stock positions — no shorts, no timing, no entry prices. What a fund held last quarter is a research lead, not a buy signal. Educational only, never financial advice.
