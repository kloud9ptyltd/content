The stat to remember

2015–2023 all together: about +$90B — with net selling in five of nine years. Since the start of 2024: roughly $1.3–1.5 trillion. The world bought more US stock in ~2.5 years than in the entire previous decade — times ten.

Why it feeds itself

1 · US stocks outperform

AI, earnings, liquidity — the S&P 500 and Nasdaq lead the world's markets.

2 · So the world index gets more American

The US is now roughly two-thirds of global stock indexes, up from about half a decade ago.

3 · So every global saver auto-buys more US

A pension in Sweden, a super fund in Sydney — every contribution to a "world tracker" routes ~66 cents per dollar into America, automatically.

4 · Which lifts US stocks. Loop.

Flows lift prices → the index weight grows → the flows grow. Nobody opted in; everyone's in it. Foreigners now hold ~18% of the entire US market (~$18–20T) — the highest share since 1945.

⚠ The other side

The marginal buyer of US stocks now lives abroad, and foreign flows are historically momentum-chasing and currency-sensitive — they were net sellers five years out of nine last decade. The thing to watch: the first negative quarter. Not a prediction — a dependency worth knowing about. Educational only, not financial advice.
