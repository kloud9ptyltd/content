Six transcripts · One message

Big Tech just told you
what's scarce.

We read all six Q2-season earnings transcripts — Microsoft, Apple, Alphabet, Amazon, Nvidia and Meta — plus their SEC filings. Every single CEO and CFO said a version of the same sentence: demand exceeds supply, and it will for years. Roughly $700B of 2026 capex is flowing through their balance sheets onto someone else's income statement. This is the map of where it lands.

02

Eight hidden signals

SIGNAL 1Memory is the acute shortage — and it's a three-player oligopoly

Amazon raised 2026 capex $200B → $220B citing memory prices as the reason. Apple called it "a 100-year flood… exponential increases," raised iPad/Mac prices over it, and noted DRAM has only three suppliers. Amazon also named hard drives and SSDs as inflated. More than 100% of Apple's gross-margin decline this quarter and next is memory.

→ MU · SK HYNIX · SAMSUNG · (storage: SNDK/WDC · STX)

SIGNAL 2Used compute is appreciating

Nvidia disclosed H100 rental prices +20% year-to-date — a 3-year-old chip going UP in price (A100 +15%). Meta gets offers at "multiples" of what it paid for its fleet. Rising rents on sunk fleets means expanding returns for anyone who owns GPUs at yesterday's prices.

→ NVDA · CRWV · NBIS · ORCL

SIGNAL 3Hyperscalers have become neocloud customers

Alphabet is explicitly renting third-party capacity as a "bridging strategy" — accepting margin pressure to do it. Meta says cloud deals are its fastest route to 2026–27 capacity. The richest companies on earth are now bidding for neocloud inventory: that's a demand floor under the whole category.

→ CRWV · NBIS · ORCL

SIGNAL 4Networking grew 199% — the quietest big number of the season

Nvidia's data-center networking line grew +199% YoY ($14.8B/quarter) — roughly 2.6× faster than its compute line. Every AI cluster is increasingly a networking and optics story.

→ MRVL · COHR · LITE · ALAB · CRDO

SIGNAL 5The power race now has hard commitments

Microsoft added a gigawatt in one quarter and is on track to double total capacity in two years. Amazon is doubling its power capacity by end-2027. Meta is building a 1GW Texas campus with BlackRock. Nvidia's CFO frames customers as "constrained by power and capital."

→ CEG · VST · GEV · TLN · OKLO · VRT

SIGNAL 6Apple just told you TSMC is full

Cook: the constraint is "advanced nodes" — a demand-forecast miss, not a supply failure, with "less flexibility in the supply chain than normal." When TSMC's #1 customer is scrambling for wafers, the foundry holds the pricing power.

→ TSM · (tools: ASML · AMAT · LRCX · KLA)

SIGNAL 7Follow the cash: hyperscaler FCF ≈ zero

Amazon's trailing free cash flow is negative $7.6B. Alphabet's quarter was FCF-negative with debt up ~$16B → ~$100B in twelve months. Meta's FCF is near zero with $84B of debt and private-credit JV backstops. The spenders are converting balance sheets into suppliers' revenue — in a shortage, own the toll collectors.

→ the entire map below

SIGNAL 8The tell: they're building exit ramps

Microsoft's CFO volunteered that "all of us are reading 1873" — the railroad-overbuild panic — and stressed capex is pivoting to short-lived assets so spending can slow fast. Meta is deferring chip purchases for "flexibility." The builders are hedging their own boom; oligopolies with pricing power hold up longest if the cycle cracks.

THE HONEST LINE

The same executives shouting "shortage" are structuring their spending so they can stop fast. If AI demand cracks, the most-levered, longest-duration pure plays get hit first. Size positions accordingly.
