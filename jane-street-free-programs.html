01 · Program

FTTP: a free trip to a Jane Street office

Who it's forA select group of first-year undergraduates planning to study math, physics, CS or another STEM field. No prior finance knowledge required.
What you getJane Street covers travel to and from, meals, housing, social events and a set daily amount for expenses. A few days of classes plus a team-based mock trading simulation game.
Key date / statusLondon: March 21–25, 2027, apply by Sunday, January 3, 2027, 11:59pm GMT (earliest deadline). New York: March 21–25, 2027, apply by Sunday, January 31, 2027, 11:59pm EST. Hong Kong: March 21–24, 2027, apply by Sunday, January 31, 2027, 11:59pm HKT. Apply only to the location nearest you.
Official FTTP page ↗
Prep project · this week, about 2 hours

Prep project

Get a feel for what the program covers: probability, expected value and trading with a team. Do 5 puzzles, then play a mock market with friends.

  1. Paste the prompt below into ChatGPT or Claude and work through the 5 puzzles. Write your own answer before asking for the solution.
  2. Gather 3+ friends and play the mock market game the prompt describes, 3 rounds.
  3. Write down one mistake you made and what you would change next round.
Copy into ChatGPT or Claude
I'm a first-year student preparing for a trading-style program application. I have no finance background. Do two things.

1. Give me 5 probability and expected-value puzzles, easiest first (coin flips, dice games, simple bets). Give one at a time. Wait for my answer, then tell me if I'm right and explain the logic simply before the next one.

2. Then write the rules for a mock market-making game I can play with 3-6 friends using paper and a deck of cards. One player is the market maker who quotes a bid and an ask on the total of cards held by the group; others buy or sell. Include how to score each round and how to tell who made money.

Keep everything beginner-friendly.

Done when: you solved the 5 puzzles and wrote down one lesson from a mock-market round.

02 · Program

WiSE 2027: a multi-day program for women and gender-expansive students

Who it's forWomen, transgender, and/or gender-expansive individuals about to start their first year of university. No finance knowledge needed.
What you getA challenging multi-day program on probability, logic, financial markets and algorithmic trading, and you meet traders and technologists. Jane Street covers travel, meals, housing, social events and a daily amount.
Key date / status2027 dates not announced yet. Use the notify form to hear first. Apply to ONE location: the region where you will be based this summer.
Official WiSE page ↗
Prep project · this week, about 2 hours

Prep project

Practise the kinds of thinking the program covers: probability, logic and simple market games. Do 5 puzzles, then play a mock market with friends.

  1. Sign up on the WiSE notify form ↗ so you hear when 2027 opens.
  2. Paste the prompt below into ChatGPT or Claude and solve the puzzles one at a time.
  3. Play the mock market game with friends for 3 rounds and note who profited and why.
Copy into ChatGPT or Claude
I'm about to start university and I'm preparing for a program on probability, logic and trading. I have no finance background. Do two things.

1. Give me 5 logic and probability puzzles, easiest first, including at least two expected-value problems. One at a time: wait for my answer, then check it and explain the reasoning in plain English.

2. Teach me what a market maker does in 5 sentences, then write the rules for a mock market-making game I can play with 3-6 friends using paper and dice. Include how to score each round.

Keep it friendly and simple.

Done when: you have signed up for the notify form and solved the 5 puzzles without looking at the answers first.

03 · Program

Quantile 2027: India's quant championship with no answer key

Who it's forAny student at a college in India (any level, any subject), plus graduates of Indian institutions within the last year. India only. Free and individual.
What you getA competition run by IQCS with a ₹1.5 lakh prize pool; Jane Street is Founding Title Sponsor. It is not a finance-knowledge, programming or paper-trading contest.
Key date / statusRegistration is not open yet ("Registration opens soon"); add your email on the site to be notified. Rounds: Spark 17 Jan (online), Orbit Feb (IIT centres), Nova Mar (IIT Kanpur).
Quantile site ↗
Prep project · this week, about 90 minutes

Prep project

There is no answer key, so practise reasoning under uncertainty: estimate, state your assumptions and defend your number.

  1. Add your email on the Quantile site so you are notified when registration opens.
  2. Paste the prompt below into ChatGPT or Claude and answer each problem with a number and your reasoning, before seeing any solution.
  3. Ask for critique of your assumptions, not for the right answer.
Copy into ChatGPT or Claude
Give me 5 estimation and probability problems in the style of a quant competition with no answer key (for example, estimating a quantity from scratch, or a probability puzzle with unclear information). One at a time.

For each one: wait for my answer and reasoning. Do NOT give me a final answer. Instead, critique my assumptions, point out what I ignored, and tell me whether my reasoning is sound and how I could check my estimate. After all 5, summarise my strengths and weaknesses.

Done when: you have answered 5 problems with a number, a range and written assumptions, and revised at least one after critique.

04 · Program

Protocol Emulator ASIC Competition: design a real chip

Who it's forStudents and professionals are both welcome. Enter solo or as a team (no size limit); one entry per team.
What you getDesign an open-source protocol emulator ASIC (a tiny CPU for reading and writing pins with precise timing) on IHP 130nm via Tiny Tapeout. Jane Street pays to tape out around four designs, and winners get their chip on a Tiny Tapeout demo board.
Key date / statusDeadline: January 18, 2027. Selections in early February. Submit a public repo with RTL, tests and a passing GDS/precheck. The submission form is not live yet. Questions: asic-competition@janestreet.com
Official contest post ↗
Prep project · this week, 2-3 hours

Prep project

Get a tiny design through the Tiny Tapeout flow before you attempt the real thing: a small CPU that toggles pins, with a testbench.

  1. Start from the Tiny Tapeout IHP Verilog template (find it via the contest post).
  2. Paste the prompt below into ChatGPT or Claude to write a tiny pin-toggling CPU and its testbench.
  3. Run the testbench in a simulator and check the precheck passes in the template workflow.
Copy into ChatGPT or Claude
Help me build a first hardware project in Verilog, starting from the Tiny Tapeout IHP Verilog template. I'm a beginner.

1. Design a tiny CPU with a 4-instruction set: SET pin, CLEAR pin, WAIT n cycles, and JUMP. It should drive an 8-bit output pin register from a small program memory.
2. Write the Verilog module that fits the Tiny Tapeout template's top-level ports.
3. Write a testbench that loads a program toggling one pin every 4 clock cycles and checks the output waveform.
4. Explain how to run the simulation with an open-source simulator, and explain each part of the code in plain English.

Done when: your testbench passes, the pin toggles on the exact cycles you programmed, and the template precheck runs without errors.
