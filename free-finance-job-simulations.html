02 · How it works

Four steps, a few hours.

  • 1

    Open a link and tap "Start Free Simulation". Forage asks you to create a free account. That's the only signup.

  • 2

    Do the tasks at your own pace. Each one opens with a short brief and videos from the company's team, and you can compare your work with an example answer when you finish.

  • 3

    Get your certificate. Complete every task and Forage gives you a certificate of completion.

  • 4

    Put it to work. Add it to LinkedIn under Licenses & certifications and to your résumé, and use what you built as an interview story: "I valued a TMT company for Bank of America's simulation…"

04 · Good to know

What you get, honestly.

It's a simulation, not an internship. Nobody is hiring you by finishing it, but it shows initiative and gives you real things to talk about.
The certificate is a certificate of completion, issued through Forage. It proves you did the work, not that the bank certified you.
"Hiring now" is Forage's own tag on the Bank of America and Standard Chartered pages. For actual open roles, check each firm's careers page.
Fidelity International is the non-US sister company of Fidelity Investments, so check which one you're applying to.
Do them soon. Firms retire these programmes without warning. In October 2026 JPMorgan's simulation disappeared and all of Citi's went offline. If a link stops working, tell me and I'll swap in the replacement.
