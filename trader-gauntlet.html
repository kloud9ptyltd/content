Gate 5

The traps — read this twice.

This gate has no playlist, and it's the one that decides everything. We ran the experiment ourselves: our AI desk generated and backtested 180+ strategies across four generations. Nearly all of them died. The killers, in order:

Why your backtest is lying

Fees. Our entire first generation — 87 strategies — lost money for one reason: trading too often. A 0.1% fee sounds like nothing until you pay it 300 times.

One lucky regime. A strategy that made +10% might have made all of it in three months of a trending market. Split your backtest window in half and demand profit in both halves — this single test killed most of our "winners."

Curve-fitting. If nudging one parameter 25% destroys the edge, there was never an edge — you found a coincidence. Test every parameter's neighbors before you believe anything.

Out of 180+, exactly four strategies survived all three tests. They trade paper money — not real money — while they prove themselves. That patience is the actual edge.
