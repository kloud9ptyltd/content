How to get in.

McKinsey.org runs Forward, a free 10-week learning program open in 130+ countries — no McKinsey connections, no fee. This page is the apply guide from the reel, and every fact on it is taken directly from mckinsey.org.

01

What it is.

Forward is a free, 10-week learning program from McKinsey.org. It runs roughly two hours a week through digital courses, virtual learning events, assignments and reflection exercises. It's delivered in English, with content translated into Latin American Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French and Arabic, and it's available in more than 130 countries across Africa, Asia Pacific, Central & South America, Europe, the Middle East and North America.

02

Who's eligible.

McKinsey.org lists four eligibility rules for Forward. All four need to be true.

  • ✓
    You live in an available country.

    Forward runs in 130+ countries across Africa, Asia Pacific, Central & South America, Europe, the Middle East and North America — check the English apply page for your country.

  • ✓
    You're working, have worked, or start within 12 months.

    Current work, prior work experience (internships and apprenticeships count), or a job starting within the next 12 months all qualify — final-year students fit under the 12-month rule.

  • ✓
    Under 5 years of total work experience.

    McKinsey.org makes exceptions here for people working in the nonprofit/NGO sector or in non-managerial roles.

  • ✓
    High school education or equivalent.

    No university degree required.

03

What you get.

Core Skills is built from six modules, then a Network level for alumni.

Adaptability & Resilience

Building the mindset to navigate change and setbacks.

Problem Solving

Structured approaches to break down and work through real problems.

Communicating for Impact

Sharpening how you present ideas and influence others.

Relationships & Well-being

Strengthening the people skills behind every career.

Digital and AI Essentials

A practical grounding in digital tools and AI basics.

Lead Forward Webinar

A live session with McKinsey experts, not a recorded course.

Completing Core Skills earns a personalised digital badge you can share on social media, plus entry to the global alumni network. The Network level that follows is ongoing, English only, and opens up further learning and volunteering.

04

How to apply.

  1. 1

    Open the official Forward page — mckinsey.org/our-programs/forward/overview.

  2. 2

    Choose your language. Forward is delivered in English, with content translated into Latin American Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French and Arabic.

  3. 3

    Click Apply and create an account.

  4. 4

    Complete the application form — employment status is a required field.

  5. 5

    Watch your inbox. Applications are reviewed, and you'll hear back by email.

06

The catch.

  • Completing Forward is not a route to a McKinsey job or interview — it's a skills program, not recruitment.
  • The modules are soft skills and AI basics, not financial modelling or consulting casework.
  • This cohort closes October 5, and McKinsey.org hasn't announced the next application window.
