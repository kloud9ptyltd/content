Claude Cowork · Mobile

Build your first AI agent — from your phone.

One prompt into Claude's Cowork mode and you get a working agent that researches the market and emails you a personalized morning brief — your holdings first — every weekday, automatically. No laptop. No code.

📱 Phone only ⏱ ~2 minutes 🧑‍💻 Zero code ☁️ Runs while you sleep
The Setup

1Get the Claude app

Download Claude from the App Store or Google Play and sign in. Cowork is Claude's agent workspace — it can use tools, connect services and schedule work for you.

2Open Cowork

In the app, switch to the Cowork tab. This is the mode where Claude doesn't just chat — it does the work: research, building, sending, scheduling.

3Copy the prompt below & fill 4 blanks

Your holdings, your email, your timezone, your delivery time. The gold placeholders are the only things you touch.

4Paste, send, answer its questions

Claude checks your connected tools for an email sender (it will help you connect AgentMail — a connector that can actually send mail, not just draft it), sends a live test brief to your inbox, then schedules it to run every weekday in the cloud — even with your phone off.

Heads-up: the first email can land in spam / Promotions — mark it "not spam" and whitelist the sender. That's normal for any new sending address.

📋 The prompt (copy it exactly)

This is the full agent spec — delivery setup, the 8-section brief, email-safe design, a live test send, and the recurring schedule. Tap Copy.

Set up a recurring daily market morning brief for me, delivered by email. === FILL THESE IN === - My holdings: {{HOLDINGS — e.g. AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, TSLA, JPM}} - Send to: {{YOUR_EMAIL}} - Timezone: {{TIMEZONE — e.g. US Eastern}} - Delivery time: {{TIME — e.g. 8:30 AM, weekdays only}} ===================== FIRST, ask me any clarifying questions, then: 1) DELIVERY SETUP I want this SENT to my inbox automatically (not left as a draft). Check my connected tools for an email connector that can actually SEND (e.g. AgentMail, Resend). If the only email tool can just draft (like the standard Gmail connector), tell me and help me connect a send-capable one. Create/use a dedicated sending inbox for these briefs. 2) BUILD TODAY'S BRIEF (research fresh data every run — never reuse old numbers) Include these sections, in this order: • MY PORTFOLIO (put this FIRST — most important): for each of my holdings, latest news, price action, and any upcoming catalyst (earnings, product events). Flag which holding has an event coming up. • MARKET SNAPSHOT: S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow — levels and % change. • TOP MARKET NEWS: key global headlines (US, Europe, Asia), overnight action, and the macro/Fed backdrop. • ECONOMIC CALENDAR: today's + this week's key US data releases with times and why they matter (CPI/PCE, jobs, ISM, jobless claims, Fed speakers/FOMC). • EARNINGS: notable names that just reported (with the reaction) + who reports today/this week. • GLOBAL PRICES: major world indices, commodities (gold, WTI/Brent), crypto (BTC, ETH), and FX (EUR/USD, DXY), with % change where available. • TECHNICAL LEVELS: key support/resistance and setup for the S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100. • MARKET SENTIMENT: CNN Fear & Greed reading and what it implies. 3) DESIGN Make it a clean, mobile-friendly HTML email using inline CSS and a table-based layout (email clients strip scripts and often block data-URI images). Dark theme, a colored header, section headers, a portfolio bar view, a sector snapshot, and a sentiment meter — all built with email-safe HTML/CSS so they render in Gmail. Always add a footer: "Auto-generated for information only — not investment advice. Verify live prices before acting." 4) SEND today's brief now as a live test, and confirm it landed (watch spam / Promotions on the first one — tell me to whitelist the sender). 5) SCHEDULE it to run and SEND automatically every weekday at my chosen time and timezone, using the recurring scheduled-task feature (so it runs in the cloud even when my devices are off). Note that a fixed schedule can drift with daylight-saving changes and offer to adjust at the changeover. Keep every brief tight and skimmable, and cite data sources at the end.
Why this works

✓It's a real agent, not a chat

The prompt forces the three things that make an agent an agent: it acts (sends real email through a connector), it verifies (live test send before scheduling), and it runs on a schedule in the cloud — no open app required. Swap the brief for anything: news digests, price alerts, competitor watch — the skeleton is reusable.
