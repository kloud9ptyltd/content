02 · How to take them free

Two kinds of free.

CourseHow it's freeWhat to do
MIT · YaleFully open. The whole class is online.Open the link and press play. No account needed.
Harvard · Oxford · Cambridge · ColumbiaFree to audit on edX: every lecture, for a limited time.Make a free edX account, then pick "Audit" (not the paid certificate).
  • 1

    Start with one. New to finance? Harvard's investing course or Cambridge's foundations. Want the deep version? Yale and MIT.

  • 2

    On edX, choose Audit. You get the lectures free; the certificate is an optional paid upgrade. Audit access is time-limited, so start soon after you enrol.

  • 3

    Put it on your résumé. List it under courses or skills ("Financial Markets, Yale University, Open Yale Courses") and bring what you learned into interviews.

03 · Good to know

Honest notes.

Certificates aren't free on edX. They're optional and cost extra; the learning itself is free.
Columbia's current run ends 30 Dec 2026. Enrol before then; the next run usually opens after.
MIT and Yale are the real recorded classes, so they're older (2008 and 2011) but the fundamentals haven't changed.
