Where the Part 2 numbers come from
  • Retire Early: our own maths. $2,000 a month for 25 years at 8.3% a year (the same return as Part 1's Millionaire Investor card) reaches $2M. The 4% rule on $2M is $80,000 a year. Returns are not guaranteed.
  • Options Trader: graduate trader total comp at an options market maker, US: QuantVault, Quantt.
  • AI in Finance Consultant: Citadel's US equities associate program posts a base of $125K–$135K plus a discretionary bonus (Citadel careers). First-year total comp of about $250K is reported on Wall Street Oasis. Bonuses vary with performance.
  • Wealth Manager: US Bureau of Labor Statistics, personal financial advisors, May 2025: BLS.
  • Macro Trader and Portfolio Manager: hedge fund pay surveys (HedgeFundInterview, WallStreetCareers). Hedge fund pay varies enormously with fund size and performance.
