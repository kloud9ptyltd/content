02 · The catch

Loving the product isn't enough.

Loved is not the same as profitable. Even Warren Buffett's Berkshire Hathaway and McDonald's trailed the index over these ten years.
The price you pay matters. Even a great company can be a bad deal if you buy it when everyone is already excited.
One company can fall a lot. Every name on this list has had a rough year. Putting everything in one is a big bet.
An index fund holds them all. One simple fund can own most of these companies at once, so you don't have to pick the winner.
03 · How to start

Three simple steps.

  1. 1

    Open a brokerage account that is available in your country. Check that it is regulated where you live.

  2. 2

    Start with an index fund or ETF that holds these companies, instead of betting on one stock.

  3. 3

    Buy a little every month. Small, steady and boring beats waiting for the perfect moment.
