01 · From the reel

First year of university? Start here.

Both programs are paid for by Jane Street: travel, meals, housing and social events. Neither needs a finance background.

HOW TO APPLY

Dates aren't posted yet. Open the program page and use "Sign up to be notified"; you'll get an email when applications open. Apply to one location only, the one nearest you.

01
First year of university

FOCUS

ALL EXPENSES PAIDMULTI-DAY1ST YEAR

A multi-day program to meet Jane Street's trading, software engineering, and strategy & product teams. Jane Street covers travel, meals, housing and social events, plus a daily allowance. For first-year students who've faced barriers to advanced STEM opportunities; their definition is broad (being first in your family at university counts).

First year of university · STEM majors

FTTP

ALL EXPENSES PAIDA FEW DAYSNO FINANCE NEEDED

A few days in the office: classes on how Jane Street trades and builds its technology, then a team mock-trading game. For first-year students planning to study maths, physics, computer science or another STEM subject. No finance knowledge needed.

03
Just finished high school · US

AMP

NO COST5 WEEKS · NYC$5,000 SCHOLARSHIP

A five-week summer program in New York (28 Jun to 30 Jul 2027) in advanced maths and computer science. Tuition, housing, meals and travel are covered, and everyone who completes it gets a $5,000 scholarship. For 2027 US high-school graduates who've faced barriers to STEM opportunities; must be able to work in the US without sponsorship.

04 · Good to know

Honest notes.

FOCUS, AMP and IN FOCUS are for students who've faced barriers to STEM opportunities. Jane Street's definition is broad; read each page and apply if it fits you.
These programs don't guarantee an internship. They get you inside the firm, teach you how it works and put you in front of the people who hire.
IN FOCUS has moved to May 2027. Applications reopen later; sign up on its page ↗ to hear first.
