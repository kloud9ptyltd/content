One a day. Start Monday.

Every link is free, with no signup. Five are on YouTube, from the official Harvard, MIT, Jane Street and freeCodeCamp channels. Thursday is my own project guide.

02 · After the week

Turn the week into a path.

  • 1

    Finish the maths. Stat 110 (34 lectures) and 18.06 are the two full courses worth completing. Do the problem sets, not just the videos.

  • 2

    Build one project properly. Pick one from my 5 quant projects, put it on GitHub and write up what you found.

  • 3

    Practise the interview. Rewatch the Jane Street mock and read their own interview guide ↗. Do mental maths and probability puzzles daily.

  • 4

    Go deeper on finance maths. The full MIT 18.S096 course ↗ has lecture notes and every video, free.

03 · Good to know

Honest notes.

"1 week" is a sampler, not a degree. Each day is one lecture or one course to see if the topic clicks.
The Harvard and MIT lectures are older recordings. The maths hasn't changed; the camera quality has.
freeCodeCamp courses use specific Python libraries and data sources. If a step breaks, the idea still holds; ask Claude to update the code.
